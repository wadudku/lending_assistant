**In short:** Conditions are recorded as structured items, shown to the field officer at verification and evidenced before the application can be approved; an unmet condition halts it and refers it back to the approver.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-327 (Must, BRD 6.6, PDF page 38) says where the approver sanctions subject to conditions, those conditions are recorded as structured items, presented to the field officer at verification, and evidenced before the application can reach approved status. An unmet condition halts the application and refers it back to the approver. This is an origination rule (the LOS side of the BRD), not a servicing rule.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that a condition written in a free note is easy to overlook, but a structured item can be checked and must be cleared.

## Example

An approver sanctions on condition that the borrower shows proof of land ownership. The field officer sees the condition at the visit and uploads the proof. Without it, the application stops and goes back to the approver.

## Terms to know

- **Condition:** something the borrower must do or show before approval.
- **Structured item:** a condition recorded as a separate, checkable entry.
- **Evidenced:** supported by a document or record.
- **Approved status:** the status after which the loan may proceed.
