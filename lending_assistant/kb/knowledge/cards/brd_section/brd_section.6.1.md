**In short:** Section 6.1 holds seven rules for setting up a partner and each partner and product pair: its details, its limits, suspension, exposure ceiling, its users, risk-sharing terms and a performance view.

## What this section says

BRD 6.1 (PDF page 29) says partner-sourced origination only works if the commercial relationship is modelled in the system. It holds seven requirements, BR-LOS-100 to BR-LOS-106. In short: a partner organisation is recorded with its legal and commercial details (100); each partner and product pair has its own limits, rates and fields (101); a suspended partner can look but not send new batches (102); a submission that would break the partner's exposure ceiling is stopped (103); partner administrators manage only their own organisation's users (104, a Should); a partner's first-loss guarantee terms are recorded for servicing to use (105, a Should); and partner performance is visible on one partner record (106).

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD gives the reason in the section introduction: exposure ceilings, batch limits and permitted products are commercial terms, and the platform must enforce them rather than rely on operational vigilance (PDF page 29).

## How it meets Frappe Lending

The section holds 7 requirements (BR-LOS-100 to BR-LOS-106). The team has not analysed them: the analysis covered the 103 servicing requirements of BRD section 7, so this card gives no verdict. Each row has a concept card with the rule and a not-analysed bridge card with pointers only.
