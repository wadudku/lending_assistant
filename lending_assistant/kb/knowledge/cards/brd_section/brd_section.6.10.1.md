**In short:** Section 6.10.1 holds four requirements for what Bank staff can see and do: full application detail, search, holds and an exceptions workspace.

## What this section says

BRD 6.10.1 (PDF page 44) holds four requirements, BR-LOS-210 to BR-LOS-213, all Must. They cover full application detail for Bank staff (210), search across all applications (211), holds on an application, a batch or a partner (212) and the exceptions workspace (213).

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that Bank staff are the ones who investigate and step in, so they need the whole record, a way to find an application and a way to stop one.

## How it meets Frappe Lending

The team has not analysed the LOS (the origination system, BRD section 6), so this section carries no verdict. The analysis was limited to the 103 servicing requirements in BRD section 7. None of the four rows is among the 103 requirements the team verified.
