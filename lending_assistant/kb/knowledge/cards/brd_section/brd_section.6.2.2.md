**In short:** Section 6.2.2 lists the 16 fields of a Phase 1 agricultural application and the business check for each; the BRD says the list will be revised.

## What this section says

BRD 6.2.2 (PDF page 31) has a table of 16 fields with type and business validation. They are: applicant name (compared to the CBS account title under BR-LOS-120); father's or spouse's name; mother's name; NID number (10, 13 or 17 digits); date of birth (age within the permitted range at application date); gender; mobile number (11 digits, valid Bangladeshi operator prefix); present address; permanent address (may be flagged the same as present); monthly income (greater than zero); bank account number (validated against CBS under BR-LOS-120); requested amount (within the partner and product ticket range); requested tenor (within the permitted set for the product); loan purpose (mapped to Bangladesh Bank agricultural credit purpose codes); partner customer reference; and consent reference (mandatory; see BR-LOS-117). The BRD adds that the field list will be revised before the project commences, according to NRB Bank's KYC standards and the data required for Bangladesh Bank CIB contribution.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD gives the reason for the revision note in the section: the list has to follow NRB Bank's KYC standards and the data needed for CIB contribution to Bangladesh Bank (PDF page 31).

## How it meets Frappe Lending

This section holds no requirement row, so there is nothing in it to compare with Frappe Lending, and the team has made no statement about it. It describes the origination system (LOS), which the team has not analysed.
