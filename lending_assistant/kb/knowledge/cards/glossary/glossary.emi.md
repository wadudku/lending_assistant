**In short:** The same amount paid every month, made of part principal and part interest.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines EMI as equated monthly installment (BRD 1.5). The plain-English guide explains it as the same amount paid every month, covering part principal and part interest.

## Example

Rahim borrows 100,000 and pays 9,000 every month. Early on, most of the 9,000 is interest; later, most is principal. The total stays the same.

## Why it matters here

BR-LMS-110 asks for several schedule methods, including monthly equated instalment. The verification confirmed five schedule methods exist in Frappe Lending (BR-LMS-110 is covered).
