**In short:** Section 14 gives 48 business conditions for accepting each capability, written in Given/When/Then form, 30 for origination and 18 for servicing.

## What this section says

BRD 14 (PDF page 75) opens: “The criteria below are the business conditions for accepting each major capability. They are written so that a business tester, not a developer, can determine whether the condition is met. Detailed test cases derive from the requirements in Sections 6 to 9.” It has two sub-sections: 14.1 Origination with 30 conditions and 14.2 Servicing with 18, making 48. Each is in the form Given / When / Then. The team has not tested these acceptance criteria and does not say that any of them is met.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD gives its reason in the opening: the conditions are “written so that a business tester, not a developer, can determine whether the condition is met” (PDF page 75).

## How it meets Frappe Lending

This section holds no requirement row, so there is nothing in it to compare with Frappe Lending, and the team has made no statement about it. The team has not tested the acceptance criteria and says nothing here about whether any is met.
