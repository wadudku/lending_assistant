**In short:** Section 1.2 is a heading only; what is in and out of scope is in its two sub-sections.

## What this section says

BRD 1.2 (PDF page 9) is a title with no text of its own. The scope is set out in 1.2.1 In scope (PDF page 9) and 1.2.2 Explicitly out of scope (PDF page 10). This section holds no requirement. Read together, the two sub-sections draw the edge of the project: the first names the systems and functions the Bank is asking for, and the second names what the platform will not do and what the Bank must supply itself.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that scope is easier to read when 'in' and 'out' are kept apart.

## How it meets Frappe Lending

This heading has no text of its own and no requirement row, so there is nothing to compare with Frappe Lending, and the team has made no statement about it.
