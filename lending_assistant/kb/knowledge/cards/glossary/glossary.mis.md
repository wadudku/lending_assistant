**In short:** Management information system: management reports. The BRD's Section 8 is headed with it.

## Plain meaning

The plain-English guide gives the full form of MIS as Management Information System and its plain meaning as management reports, with a pointer to BRD Section 8. The BRD itself never spells the three letters out: they appear only in the heading of Section 8, “Reporting, MIS and regulatory obligations” (PDF page 57), and in the contents entry for it.

## Example

Every Monday a Finance officer opens a one-page summary of how many loans were paid out last week, how many repayments are overdue and how much is waiting for approval. That summary is MIS.

## Why it matters here

BRD Section 8 is the part of the BRD that deals with reporting. It has four sub-sections and 16 requirement rows, on origination and servicing reports, Bangladesh Bank returns and reconciliation. The BRD's opening paragraph for the section says reporting is “not a by-product of this programme”.
