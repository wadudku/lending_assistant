**In short:** The national identity database against which an applicant's identity is checked.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines ECDB as the Election Commission Database, the national identity database against which applicant identity is verified (BRD 1.5).

## Example

An applicant's name, date of birth and NID are sent to the ECDB. The reply says whether they agree, and returns a portrait photo that is kept for face matching later (BRD 5.1, stages 4 and 9).

## Why it matters here

Identity verification against the ECDB is stage 4 of the process (BRD 5.1) and a failure blocks sanction (BR-LOS-332). The team has analysed only the LMS requirements (Section 7). The LOS requirements have not been analysed.
