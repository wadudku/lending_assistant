**In short:** Section 9 sets out how the platform reaches the outside systems it depends on, from the core banking system to the SMS gateway.

## What this section says

BRD 9 (PDF page 60) opens the integration requirements. It holds seven numbered sub-sections, 9.1 to 9.7, and 42 requirement rows in all (12 in 9.2.1, 13 in 9.3, 3 in 9.4, 4 in 9.5, 5 in 9.6 and 5 in 9.7). Sections 9, 9.1 and 9.2 have no row of their own. Section 9 has no introductory paragraph; its first content is the inventory in 9.1.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that the platform depends on systems it does not control, so each connection has to be listed and specified. Section 9 has no introduction, and the BRD's reasons are given in 9.2 and in individual rows.

## How it meets Frappe Lending

The team's analysis covered Section 7 only. This section was not analysed, so the team makes no statement about it. For the Section 7 totals, see `brd_section.7`.
