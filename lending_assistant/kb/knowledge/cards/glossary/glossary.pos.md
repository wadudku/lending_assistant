**In short:** The principal still owed on a loan. One co-lending schedule type is built on it.

## Plain meaning

The plain-English guide defines POS as principal outstanding: the principal still owed. It adds that one co-lending schedule type is called POS reduction plus interest.

## Example

Rahim borrowed 100,000 and has repaid 30,000 of principal. His POS is 70,000.

## Why it matters here

It appears in the guide as the name of a co-lending schedule type, POS reduction plus interest.
