**In short:** The system that handles a loan before payout: application, scoring, checks, approval and disbursement.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines the LOS as the Loan Origination System, covering application through to confirmed disbursement (BRD 1.5). The plain-English guide says it handles the loan before the payout and is Section 6 of the BRD.

## Example

Rahim applies for a loan, is scored and approved in the LOS. When the money reaches his account, the LOS hands the funded loan to the LMS.

## Why it matters here

The team has analysed only the LMS requirements (Section 7). The LOS requirements have not been analysed.
