**In short:** Section 6.7 is the BRD's section on the field officer's work: it opens with the operating context and holds four sub-sections with 27 requirements.

## What this section says

BRD 6.7 (PDF page 39) opens with a short paragraph and then has four sub-sections. It holds no requirement rows of its own. The paragraph says field officers work at gatherings arranged by the partner, often in rural places with unreliable mobile data, that they stand rather than sit and work under time pressure with a queue of applicants waiting, and that they handle applicants who may have limited literacy. The sub-sections are 6.7.1 Operating context and device (6 requirements), 6.7.2 Working without connectivity (5), 6.7.3 Verification and evidence (9) and 6.7.4 Assignment and officer management (7), 27 requirements in all.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD gives the reason on PDF page 39: "Field officers work at gatherings arranged by the partner, frequently in rural locations with unreliable mobile data. They stand rather than sit, work under time pressure with a queue of applicants waiting, and handle applicants who may have limited literacy. Every requirement in this section follows from that operating context."

## How it meets Frappe Lending

The team has not analysed the LOS (the origination system, BRD section 6), so this section carries no verdict. The analysis was limited to the 103 servicing requirements in BRD section 7. None of the 27 rows is among the 103 requirements the team verified.
