**In short:** Sanctioned applications appear in a queue that can be grouped by partner, by district and upazila, and by scheduled meeting event.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-180 (Must, BRD 6.7.4, PDF page 41) says "sanctioned applications shall appear in an assignment queue, groupable by partner, by district and upazila, and by scheduled meeting event". Three ways of grouping the queue are named: partner, district with upazila, and meeting event.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that assigners need to see which sanctioned applicants are waiting for a visit and to group them by where and when officers will meet them.

## Example

Sanctioned applications from one partner wait in the queue. The person assigning them groups the queue by scheduled meeting event and sees all the applications that belong to next Tuesday's meeting together.

## Terms to know

- **Assignment queue:** the list of sanctioned applications waiting to be assigned to an officer.
- **Meeting event:** the BRD's glossary: a gathering arranged by a partner at which eligible applicants and Bank field officers are both present.
