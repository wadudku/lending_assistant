**In short:** An approved period during which some payments are put off. How interest is treated in that period follows the product's terms.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines a moratorium as an approved period during which some payment obligations are deferred, with interest treatment following product terms (BRD 1.5).

## Example

Rahim has a 3-month moratorium after payout. He pays no instalment in those 3 months. Whether interest still builds up depends on how the product is set up.

## Why it matters here

BR-LMS-112 sets the rules for a payment holiday. After verification the result for BR-LMS-112 is partly covered; the baseline code reading had called it covered.
