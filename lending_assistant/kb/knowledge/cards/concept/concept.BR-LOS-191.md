**In short:** The officer's device must never receive, display, cache or log any bureau detail: not the score, exposure figures, institution names, classification or account counts.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-191 (Must, BRD 6.8, PDF page 42) says the device "shall never receive, display, cache or log any bureau detail". The BRD lists five things that are covered: "no score, no exposure figures, no institution names, no classification, no account counts". The four verbs are receive, display, cache and log. So the bureau detail must not reach the device at all, which is stronger than hiding it on screen.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD gives the reason on PDF page 42: "Bureau data concerning an individual's borrowings at other institutions must not be exposed to a field officer standing in a village meeting, nor cached on a device that may be lost."

## Example

An officer's device is lost after a village meeting. Because the device never received any bureau detail, there is no score, exposure figure, institution name, classification or account count on it to be found.

## Terms to know

- **Bureau detail:** the BRD's list: score, exposure figures, institution names, classification and account counts.
- **Cache:** one of the four verbs in the row, with receive, display and log; the BRD does not define it.
