**In short:** Chapter 5 describes the end-to-end process: the narrative, the origination states, the servicing states and the handoff control.

## What this section says

BRD 5 (PDF page 24) is a heading with no text of its own. Its sections are 5.1 Process narrative (PDF pages 24 and 25), 5.2 Origination lifecycle states (PDF pages 25 to 27), 5.3 Servicing lifecycle states (PDF pages 27 and 28) and 5.4 The handoff control (PDF page 28). Sections 5.2, 5.3 and 5.4 hold requirement rows. Read in order, the chapter moves from the whole story, to the status model of each system, to the single control between them.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that a reader should see the full path of a loan before reading the requirements for each step.

## How it meets Frappe Lending

This heading has no text of its own and no requirement row, so there is nothing to compare with Frappe Lending, and the team has made no statement about it.
