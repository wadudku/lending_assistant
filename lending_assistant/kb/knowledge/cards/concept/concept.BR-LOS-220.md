**In short:** Partner users can list, filter, sort and export their own applications, with six named filters.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-220 (Must, BRD 6.10.2, PDF page 44) says partner users "shall be able to list, filter, sort and export their own applications". The filters "shall include batch, status, submission date range, amount band, district and assigned field officer". That is six named filters, and the word "include" means the list is not closed.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that a partner with many applications needs to find the ones it is waiting on, and to take the list into its own records.

## Example

A partner user filters their applications by batch and status, sorts them by submission date and exports the result.

## Terms to know

- **Amount band:** one of the six filters the BRD names; it does not give the bands.
- **Assigned field officer:** the officer an application is assigned to (see BR-LOS-182 and BR-LOS-183).
