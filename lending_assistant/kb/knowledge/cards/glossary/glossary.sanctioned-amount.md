**In short:** The amount the bank agrees to lend, which can be lower than the amount the applicant asked for.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines the sanctioned amount as the amount the Bank agrees to lend, which may be lower than the amount requested (BRD 1.5).

## Example

A borrower asks for 200,000 and is sanctioned 150,000. The sanctioned amount is 150,000; both numbers are kept (BR-LOS-002).

## Why it matters here

Where it is lower than the request, both figures are kept and shown (BR-LOS-002). Servicing receives it as part of the approved terms in the handoff (BRD 3.2). The team has analysed only the LMS requirements (Section 7). The LOS requirements have not been analysed.
