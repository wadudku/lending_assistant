**In short:** The notification channels and the full set of borrower notice templates are to be confirmed by the bank.

## What the rule says

BR-INT-054 (Must, BRD 9.7, PDF page 65) says the notification channels and the full set of borrower notice templates are to be confirmed by the bank. BR-INT-051 lists the five events that need a borrower notice, so the bank's confirmation would cover the wording of those notices and the channels they travel on.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that the supplier cannot decide which channels the bank uses or what its notices say, so the bank must supply them.

## Example

The bank states that notices go by SMS only, and sends the approved wording of each notice in Bangla and English. The supplier configures exactly those.

## Terms to know

- **Channel:** the route a message takes, such as SMS or email.
- **Template:** a message layout with blanks filled in for each borrower.
