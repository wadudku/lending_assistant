**In short:** Chapter 4 covers stakeholders, roles and governance: the stakeholder register, origination and servicing roles, and access control.

## What this section says

BRD 4 (PDF page 21) is a heading with no text of its own. Its sections are 4.1 Stakeholder register (PDF page 21), 4.2 Business roles (PDF pages 21 and 22, with 4.2.1 Origination roles and 4.2.2 Servicing roles) and 4.3 Access control and segregation of duties (PDF pages 22 and 23). Only 4.3 holds requirement rows. Read in order, the chapter moves from who has an interest in the programme, to the roles that do the work, to the rules that keep those roles apart.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that a lending platform has many kinds of people with different powers, and the rules that govern them are clearer once the people are named.

## How it meets Frappe Lending

This heading has no text of its own and no requirement row, so there is nothing to compare with Frappe Lending, and the team has made no statement about it.
