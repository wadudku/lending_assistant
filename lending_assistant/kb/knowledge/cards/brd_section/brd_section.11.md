**In short:** Section 11 proposes four delivery phases (origination, servicing core, risk and treatment, scale and extension), each with scope and a business outcome.

## What this section says

BRD 11 (PDF page 69) proposes four phases on the principle that the bank should originate and service a live book end to end as early as possible, “at limited volume and with a small number of partners”. Phase 1, Origination, includes the partner portal, admin console, field officer app, scoring engine, sanction workflow, bureau and eKYC integration, the automation layer for account validation, loan account opening and disbursement, origination reporting and daily reconciliation. The outcome is agricultural micro loan origination live with a small number of partners. Phase 2, Servicing core, includes handoff and booking, schedules, demands and charges, accrual, payment allocation, suspense, statements and opening-balance migration. Repayment collection and detection are added once the bank has decided them, and until then this phase cannot be committed to a date. Phase 3, Risk and treatment, includes DPD, classification and provisioning to Bangladesh Bank rules, income suspense, collections, restructuring, write-off, collateral and regulatory extracts. Phase 4, Scale and extension, includes bulk repricing, scorecard recalibration, co-lending, further products, a borrower-facing channel, partner commission and Bangla in web interfaces. A boxed note says phasing is “indicative and subject to the Bank's priorities”. The section gives no calendar dates.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD gives its reason in the first paragraph: the bank should be able to originate and service a live book end to end as early as possible, “rather than waiting for the complete feature set before any loan flows through the platform” (PDF page 69).

## How it meets Frappe Lending

This section holds no requirement row, so there is nothing in it to compare with Frappe Lending, and the team has made no statement about it. The section places each part of the BRD in a phase; it does not say which parts Frappe Lending already has.
