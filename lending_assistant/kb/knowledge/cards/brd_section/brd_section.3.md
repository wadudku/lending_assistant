**In short:** Chapter 3 gives the solution overview: how lending is split between the two systems, who owns what, the system landscape and the guiding principles.

## What this section says

BRD 3 (PDF page 17) is a heading with no text of its own. Its sections are 3.1 Two systems, one lending lifecycle (PDF page 17), 3.2 The ownership boundary (PDF pages 17 and 18), 3.3 System landscape (PDF pages 18 and 19) and 3.4 Guiding business principles (PDF pages 19 and 20). Only 3.4 holds requirement rows. Read in order, the chapter moves from the split of the lifecycle, to the rule at the split, to the layers that make up the platform, to the principles every design must obey.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that the reader should see the whole shape of the solution before the detailed requirements begin.

## How it meets Frappe Lending

This heading has no text of its own and no requirement row, so there is nothing to compare with Frappe Lending, and the team has made no statement about it.
