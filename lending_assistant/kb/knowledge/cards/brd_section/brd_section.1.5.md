**In short:** Section 1.5 is the BRD's list of terms and abbreviations, from Advance payment to Write-off.

## What this section says

BRD 1.5 (PDF pages 11 to 13) is a two-column table of terms and definitions, in alphabetical order from Advance payment to Write-off. It includes banking words (Demand, DPD, Value date, Waiver), abbreviations of Bangladeshi institutions (CIB, ECDB) and platform words (Application, Batch, Field Officer, Meeting event, Sanctioned amount, Scorecard, Score cut-off). This knowledge base turns these terms into glossary cards; those that are still being written are not linked here.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that a shared glossary keeps the Bank, the supplier and the regulator using each word in the same way.

## How it meets Frappe Lending

This section holds no requirement row, so there is nothing in it to compare with Frappe Lending, and the team has made no statement about it.
