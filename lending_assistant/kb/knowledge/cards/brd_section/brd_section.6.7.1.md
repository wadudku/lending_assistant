**In short:** Section 6.7.1 holds six requirements for the field officer's app: Android, one device per officer, two-factor login, two languages, one-handed use and no screenshots.

## What this section says

BRD 6.7.1 (PDF pages 39 and 40) holds six requirements, BR-LOS-150 to BR-LOS-155. Five are Must and one is Should (BR-LOS-155). They cover Android as the platform (150), a device bound to a registered officer (151), login with a second factor and an idle lock (152), Bangla and English (153), one-handed use while standing (154) and no screenshots of identity data or evidence (155).

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD gives the reason for the whole of 6.7 on PDF page 39: "Field officers work at gatherings arranged by the partner, frequently in rural locations with unreliable mobile data. They stand rather than sit, work under time pressure with a queue of applicants waiting, and handle applicants who may have limited literacy. Every requirement in this section follows from that operating context."

## How it meets Frappe Lending

The team has not analysed the LOS (the origination system, BRD section 6), so this section carries no verdict. The analysis was limited to the 103 servicing requirements in BRD section 7. None of the six rows is among the 103 requirements the team verified. Each has a bridge card that says so.
