**In short:** Partners have a disbursed-accounts view with five items, taken from servicing.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-223 (Must, BRD 6.10.2, PDF page 44) says partners "shall have a disbursed-accounts view showing loan account number, sanctioned amount, disbursement date, tenor and instalment amount, sourced from servicing". That is five items, and the BRD says they come from servicing.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that after payout the partner needs to see what was disbursed for its applicants, and the figures come from the system that holds the loan so they are not typed twice.

## Example

A partner opens the disbursed-accounts view and sees, for each disbursed account, the loan account number, the sanctioned amount, the disbursement date, the tenor and the instalment amount.

## Terms to know

- **Servicing:** the loan management side of the BRD (section 7), after disbursement.
- **Tenor:** one of the five items the BRD lists for the view; it does not define it.
