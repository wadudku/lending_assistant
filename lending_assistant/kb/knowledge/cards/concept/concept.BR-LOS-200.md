**In short:** Before approval the application shows the sanctioned amount, tenor, indicative instalment, rate and any fees, the officer must confirm, and any difference from the requested amount is shown plainly.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-200 (Must, BRD 6.9, PDF page 43) says that before approval the application "shall display the sanctioned amount, tenor, indicative instalment, applicable rate and any fees" and "shall require explicit officer confirmation". Where the sanctioned amount differs from the amount requested, "the difference shall be shown plainly so that the officer can explain it to the applicant".

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD gives the reason for showing a difference, in the row on PDF page 43: "so that the officer can explain it to the applicant". The BRD doesn't say why the other figures are shown; the usual reason is that approval commits the bank to pay out, so the officer confirms with the figures in front of them.

## Example

An applicant asked for a larger amount than was sanctioned. Before approval the officer sees the sanctioned amount, tenor, indicative instalment, rate and fees, and sees the difference from the requested amount. The officer explains it to the applicant and then confirms.

## Terms to know

- **Sanctioned amount:** the amount the BRD says is displayed before approval.
- **Indicative instalment:** the instalment figure the BRD says is displayed; the BRD calls it indicative.
