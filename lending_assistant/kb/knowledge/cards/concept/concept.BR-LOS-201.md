**In short:** Opening the account and disbursing are two separate steps, each retryable on its own, each with a distinct platform-generated reference written into the core banking record and its own progress state.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-201 (Must, BRD 6.9, PDF page 43) says loan account opening and disbursement "shall be two distinct steps, each independently retryable, each carrying a distinct platform-generated unique reference written into the Core Banking System record, and each with its own progress state visible to the officer". So each step has its own retry, its own reference and its own progress state.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why for this row; the usual reason is that a failure in one step can then be retried without repeating the other. The BRD's context for 6.9 on PDF page 43 is that "the controlling risk is duplicate disbursement arising from an uncertain response to an instruction that has in fact already executed".

## Example

The loan account is opened and the officer sees that step as complete. The disbursement then fails and the officer sees that step as failed. Only the disbursement is retried. Each step has its own reference in the core banking record.

## Terms to know

- **Unique reference:** a reference generated by the platform, distinct for each step, as the BRD says.
- **Core Banking System:** the bank's core system; see the glossary term CBS.
- **Progress state:** where each step stands, shown to the officer.
