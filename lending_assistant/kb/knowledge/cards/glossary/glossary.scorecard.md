**In short:** The set of questions, bands, weights and cut-offs that turns an application into a score and a risk grade.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines a scorecard as the configured set of attributes, bands, weights and cut-offs that produces a credit score and risk grade for an application (BRD 1.5).

## Example

A scorecard gives points for income, tenor, district and the partner's track record. An application totals 640 points, which falls in grade B.

## Why it matters here

Every submitted application is scored (BR-LOS-300) and the scorecard is owned by Credit Risk and changed without a software release (BR-LOS-302). The team has analysed only the LMS requirements (Section 7). The LOS requirements have not been analysed.
