**In short:** A borrower's promise to pay a set amount by a set date. If the date passes without payment, it is a broken promise.

## Plain meaning

The plain-English guide defines PTP as a promise to pay: the borrower promises to pay a set amount by a set date, and if they do not, it becomes a Broken PTP.

## Example

Rahim, 40 days late, promises on the phone to pay 9,000 by Friday. The collector notes the promise. If Friday passes with nothing paid, the note becomes a broken PTP.

## Why it matters here

BR-LMS-198 asks that a collections workflow supports contact history, promises to pay and their fulfilment, follow-ups and escalation to recovery. In the verification, the collections module is develop only and not part of 16.5.1, so this is a gap.
