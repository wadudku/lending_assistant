**In short:** The portal shows a dashboard with pipeline volumes and values by status, conversion and drop-off by stage, and items awaiting partner action. This one is a Should.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-225 (Should, BRD 6.10.2, PDF page 45) says the portal "shall present a dashboard showing pipeline volumes and values by status, conversion and drop-off by stage, and items awaiting partner action". That is three things shown. Its priority is Should, not Must.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that a partner wants one place to see how its applications are moving and what needs its attention.

## Example

A partner opens the dashboard and sees the number and value of applications at each status, how many move from one stage to the next, and the items waiting for the partner to act.

## Terms to know

- **Pipeline:** the BRD's word for what the volumes and values count; it does not define it further.
- **Drop-off:** the BRD's term in "conversion and drop-off by stage"; it does not define it.
