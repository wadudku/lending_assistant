**In short:** Extra interest charged on overdue amounts, kept apart from the normal interest, under the bank's policy.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines penal interest as additional interest applied to qualifying overdue amounts under policy (BRD 1.5).

## Example

Rahim's 9,000 instalment is 10 days late. The bank adds a daily extra rate on the overdue amount for those 10 days. That extra is the penal interest.

## Why it matters here

BR-LMS-141 asks for penalty interest to be separate from normal accrual, under a configurable rate and basis. The verified result is partly covered. The verification also asks NRB which penal model it wants: a rate on the overdue amount kept separate, or one all-in overdue rate.
