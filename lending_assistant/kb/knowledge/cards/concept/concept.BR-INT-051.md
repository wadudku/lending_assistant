**In short:** Borrowers are notified on disbursement, instalment due and overdue, approved term change, settlement and closure, through configurable templates in Bangla and English.

## What the rule says

BR-INT-051 (Must, BRD 9.7, PDF page 65) says borrower notifications are issued on five events: disbursement; instalment due and overdue; an approved term change; settlement; and closure. They use configurable templates in Bangla and English.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that borrowers need to know when money has moved or a payment is due, in the language they read.

## Example

On the day after an instalment falls due and is unpaid, the borrower gets an SMS from the overdue template in Bangla, with the amount and the date.

## Terms to know

- **Borrower message:** a notice to the borrower, issued from a configurable template in Bangla and English; the channels are left to the Bank (BR-INT-054).
- **Template:** a message layout with blanks filled in for each borrower.
- **Overdue:** past the due date without payment.
