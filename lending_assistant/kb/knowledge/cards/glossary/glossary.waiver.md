**In short:** The bank's approved cancellation of an amount a borrower owes. It is not a cash payment.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines a waiver as an approved cancellation of a receivable component, and says it is not a cash payment (BRD 1.5). The bank gives up the amount; the borrower pays nothing for it.

## Example

Rahim owes 500 of penalty. The bank waives it, so he owes 0 for that penalty. No money moved, but the receivable is gone.

## Why it matters here

The BRD wants a second person and a record for each waiver. See `decision.waiver` for the rule, the approval limits and how Frappe Lending lets a single user create one today.
