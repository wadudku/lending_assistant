**In short:** Section 15 says that once the BRD is baselined, changes go through written change requests, assessed and signed, and deferred requirements go to a deferred-scope register.

## What this section says

BRD 15 (PDF page 79) says that once baselined, changes to the document are raised as change requests, assessed for impact on scope, cost and timeline, and approved by the signatories before implementation. Confirmations received against the dependencies of Section 12 that alter a stated requirement are handled as change requests. A table gives five steps: change raised in writing (the requesting party); impact assessed (Polygon); impact reviewed and a decision taken to accept, defer or reject (joint programme governance); accepted change signed (both parties); and document reissued at the next version, with the change recorded in version control (Polygon). Requirements that are deferred rather than rejected go on a deferred-scope register carried forward to the next phase. The words “End of document” that close the BRD are not a requirement.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD gives its reason for the register in the section: it exists “so that nothing agreed as needed is lost between phases” (PDF page 79). It doesn't say why change requests are needed; the usual reason is that scope, cost and timeline stay agreed by both sides.

## How it meets Frappe Lending

This section holds no requirement row, so there is nothing in it to compare with Frappe Lending, and the team has made no statement about it.
