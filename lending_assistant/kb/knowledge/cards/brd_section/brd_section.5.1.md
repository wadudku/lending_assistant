**In short:** Section 5.1 tells the agricultural micro loan story in seventeen stages, from partner setup to closure, naming the system for each.

## What this section says

BRD 5.1 (PDF pages 24 and 25) describes the Phase 1 agricultural micro loan flow in seventeen stages, with the system for each. Stages 1 to 11 are in the LOS: 1 partner and product setup; 2 application submission; 3 account validation against the Core Banking System; 4 identity verification against the Election Commission Database; 5 credit scoring; 6 sampling; 7 sanction; 8 assignment; 9 field verification; 10 credit bureau check; 11 approval and funding. Stage 12 is the handoff from the LOS to the LMS. Stages 13 to 17 are in the LMS: 13 booking and scheduling; 14 servicing; 15 risk treatment; 16 change and resolution; 17 closure. The BRD says other products vary in the channel through which an application arrives and in the verification steps, but follow the same spine. Stage 14 notes that how repayments are collected and detected is a decision reserved to the Bank (Section 7.7).

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that a numbered story gives every later requirement a place in the whole. The BRD's own note, that other products follow the same spine, shows the story is a pattern as well as one product (PDF page 24).

## How it meets Frappe Lending

This section holds no requirement row, so there is nothing in it to compare with Frappe Lending, and the team has made no statement about it. Stages 13 to 17 are the servicing stages that the team's analysis of BRD section 7 deals with. It describes the origination system (LOS), which the team has not analysed.
