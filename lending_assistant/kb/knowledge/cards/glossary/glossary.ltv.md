**In short:** The loan amount compared with the value of the collateral behind it. A high figure means thin cover.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines LTV as loan to value: exposure compared with eligible collateral value (BRD 1.5). The plain-English guide gives it as the loan amount divided by the collateral value.

## Example

Rahim borrows 80,000 against collateral worth 100,000. The LTV is 80,000 divided by 100,000, or 80 per cent. If the collateral falls to 90,000, the LTV rises to about 89 per cent.

## Why it matters here

The collateral rules of the BRD are in section 7.8, collateral and security servicing.
