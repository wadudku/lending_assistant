**In short:** Section 6.10 is a heading with two sub-sections, one for Bank oversight and one for partner visibility, holding ten requirements.

## What this section says

BRD 6.10 (PDF page 44) has no text or requirement rows of its own. It has two sub-sections: 6.10.1 Bank oversight (4 requirements) and 6.10.2 Partner visibility (6), ten requirements in all.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that the Bank and the partners need different views of the same applications, which is how the two sub-sections divide. The section has no text of its own.

## How it meets Frappe Lending

The team has not analysed the LOS (the origination system, BRD section 6), so this section carries no verdict. The analysis was limited to the 103 servicing requirements in BRD section 7. None of the ten rows is among the 103 requirements the team verified.
