**In short:** Section 10 lists the 37 business entities the two systems and the business share, in three tables: origination, servicing and shared.

## What this section says

BRD 10 (PDF page 66) opens with two sentences: “Indicative only. The logical data model is a design deliverable. This table establishes the shared vocabulary in which the two systems and the business describe the same things.” It holds three tables, in 10.1 to 10.3, with 37 entities in all: 16 origination entities, 16 servicing entities and 5 shared entities. Each table gives an entity, its purpose and its notable attributes. Section 10 has no requirement row.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD gives its reason in the opening: the table “establishes the shared vocabulary in which the two systems and the business describe the same things” (PDF page 66).

## How it meets Frappe Lending

This section holds no requirement row, so there is nothing in it to compare with Frappe Lending, and the team has made no statement about it.
