**In short:** Bangladesh Bank's credit bureau, the source of a borrower's credit history.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines CIB as the Credit Information Bureau of Bangladesh Bank, the source of borrower credit history (BRD 1.5).

## Example

Before approving a loan, the Bank asks the CIB whether the applicant has unpaid loans elsewhere. Each month the Bank also reports its own borrowers to the CIB (BRD 2.3, OBJ-08).

## Why it matters here

The bureau check is stage 10 of the process (BRD 5.1) and bureau data can feed the scorecard (BR-LOS-301). The team has analysed only the LMS requirements (Section 7). The LOS requirements have not been analysed.
