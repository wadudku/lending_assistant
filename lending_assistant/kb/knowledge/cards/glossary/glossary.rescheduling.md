**In short:** Changing the payment dates or the tenor of a loan to help a borrower who struggles to repay, or for another approved need.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines rescheduling as a change to payment dates or tenor to address repayment difficulty or approved servicing need (BRD 1.5). It changes less than a restructuring, which can also change the rate or capitalise amounts.

## Example

Rahim lost his job for two months. The bank moves his due dates later and adds 3 months to the tenor. The amounts and the rate are not touched.

## Why it matters here

BR-LMS-182 asks for rescheduling and broader restructuring, with the type recorded. BR-LMS-188 asks that a reschedule is reported to Bangladesh Bank. Frappe Lending has no separate reschedule category, so the two are not told apart in the test.
