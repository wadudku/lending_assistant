**In short:** The score at which the recommended decision changes between auto-approve, refer and decline.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines a score cut-off as the score boundary at which the recommended decision changes between auto-approve, refer and decline (BRD 1.5).

## Example

For one product, 700 and above is auto-approve, 550 to 699 is refer, and below 550 is decline. The two boundaries, 700 and 550, are the cut-offs.

## Why it matters here

Cut-offs may differ by product and by partner (BR-LOS-303) and are owned by Credit Risk (BR-LOS-302). The team has analysed only the LMS requirements (Section 7). The LOS requirements have not been analysed.
