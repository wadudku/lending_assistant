**In short:** Section 4.1 is a register of eleven stakeholders with their interest in the programme and what each owns or does.

## What this section says

BRD 4.1 (PDF page 21) is a table of eleven stakeholders, each with an interest and an involvement: Retail / MSME business (business owner; approves product configuration and phasing); Credit Risk (owns sampling policy, bureau decision rules, classification bands and provisioning percentages); Operations (assignment, meeting events and exception handling); Finance and Accounts (offset sequences, accounting mode, reconciliation closure); Collections and Recovery (recovery workflow, settlement approval limits); Internal Control and Compliance (approves audit, retention and consent treatment); Internal Audit (reviews sampling reproducibility and audit trail completeness); ICT (endpoints, credentials, test environment, security clearance); aggregator partners (external users of the Partner Portal, onboarded and governed by the Bank); the borrower (subject of the process, consent giver, recipient of notices and statements); and Bangladesh Bank (recipient of regulatory returns; sets the classification, provisioning and CIB contribution rules the configuration must express).

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that the people who must approve, supply or inspect each part of the system need to be named early.

## How it meets Frappe Lending

This section holds no requirement row, so there is nothing in it to compare with Frappe Lending, and the team has made no statement about it.
