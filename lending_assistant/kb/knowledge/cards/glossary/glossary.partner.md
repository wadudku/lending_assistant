**In short:** A third party that finds borrowers for the bank, such as an agricultural fintech or a device financier.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines a partner as a third-party aggregator sourcing borrowers, for example an agricultural fintech or a device financier (BRD 1.5).

## Example

A farm-supply fintech brings 500 farmers to the bank. The fintech is the partner.

## Why it matters here

BRD 7.12 covers co-lending and partner economics. BR-LMS-210 asks for a list of co-lending partners and is covered in Frappe Lending, which has a **Loan Partner** record. Partner reports are a gap (`devitem.D19`).
