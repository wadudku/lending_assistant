**In short:** Every inquiry is logged with eight items, including the raw response and the rule version, and kept for the full retention period.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-196 (Must, BRD 6.8, PDF page 42) says every inquiry "shall be logged with application reference, initiating officer, timestamp, borrower identifier, inquiry reference, raw response, evaluated outcome and rule version". That is eight items. The log is "retained for the full retention period". This row gives no length for that period.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that the bank can later show exactly what it asked, what came back and how it was judged.

## Example

An inquiry is made for an applicant. The log keeps the application reference, the initiating officer, the time, the borrower identifier, the inquiry reference, the raw response, the outcome and the rule version. It stays for the full retention period.

## Terms to know

- **Raw response:** the response item the BRD lists, apart from the evaluated outcome.
- **Retention period:** how long records must be kept; BR-LOS-196 gives no figure.
