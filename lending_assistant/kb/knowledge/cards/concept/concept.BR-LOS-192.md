**In short:** When the bureau check fails, the officer sees neutral next-step guidance that does not state or imply the reason.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-192 (Must, BRD 6.8, PDF page 42) says that "a fail outcome shall present neutral next-step guidance to the officer without stating or implying a reason". So the message after a fail tells the officer what to do next, and nothing in it gives away why the application failed.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that any reason shown to the officer would pass on bureau information to a person standing in a village meeting, which the BRD says on page 42 must not happen.

## Example

An application fails the bureau step. The officer's device shows a message about what to do next. The message does not say whether the cause was an overdue balance, a classification or anything else.

## Terms to know

- **Neutral next-step guidance:** guidance that does not state or imply a reason, as the BRD puts it.
