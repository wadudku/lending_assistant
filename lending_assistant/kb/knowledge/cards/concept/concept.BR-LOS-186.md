**In short:** The bank gets a report on each officer's verifications completed, average time per verification, abandonment rate, bureau pass rate and disbursement conversion.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-186 (Should, BRD 6.7.4, PDF page 42) says officer performance "shall be reported" using five measures: verifications completed, average elapsed time per verification, abandonment rate, bureau pass rate and disbursement conversion. It is a Should.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that managers use such a report to see who is completing checks, how long they take and how many of an officer's applicants reach payout.

## Example

A Bank user opens the report and sees, for each officer, the five measures: verifications completed, average time per verification, abandonment rate, bureau pass rate and disbursement conversion.

## Terms to know

- **Abandonment rate:** one of the five measures; abandoned is a verification status in BRD 5.2 (PDF page 26); BR-LOS-162 adds the purge case. The BRD gives no formula.
- **Bureau pass rate:** one of the five measures; the bureau check is in BRD 6.8. The BRD gives no formula.
- **Disbursement conversion:** one of the five measures. The BRD gives no formula.
