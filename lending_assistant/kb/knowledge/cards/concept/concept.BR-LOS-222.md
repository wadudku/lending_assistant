**In short:** Partners have no access to six kinds of data: bureau data of any kind, identity images or their scores, sampling selection or outcome detail, field officer notes, internal reference data, or another partner's data.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-222 (Must, BRD 6.10.2, PDF page 44) says "Partners shall not have access to credit bureau data of any kind, identity images or match scores, sampling selection or outcome detail, field officer notes, internal reference data, or any data belonging to another partner." That is six kinds of data.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that these items are sensitive or internal to the bank, and one partner must not see another's applicants.

## Example

A partner user opens an application. No bureau data, identity image, match score, sampling result, field officer note or internal reference data is shown, and they cannot reach an application that belongs to another partner.

## Terms to know

- **Sampling:** the BRD's glossary says: manual review of a randomly selected subset of submitted applications.
- **Internal reference data:** data the BRD says is internal; it does not list it.
