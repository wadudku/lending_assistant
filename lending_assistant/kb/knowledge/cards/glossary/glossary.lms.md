**In short:** The system that looks after a loan after the money has been paid out: schedule, interest, repayments, overdue, closure.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines the LMS as the Loan Management System, which services the loan after confirmed disbursement (BRD 1.5). The plain-English guide says it runs the loan after payout, and that Section 7 of the BRD covers it.

## Example

Rahim's loan is approved and paid out by the origination system. From that moment the LMS raises his monthly demands, accrues interest and takes his repayments.

## Why it matters here

This project compares the NRB LMS requirements in BRD Section 7 with Frappe Lending, which plays the LMS role. The origination side is the LOS.
