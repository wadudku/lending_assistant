**In short:** Bank staff can place a hold on a single application, a batch or an entire partner, which stops further progression until an investigation is done.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-212 (Must, BRD 6.10.1, PDF page 44) says Bank staff "shall be able to place a hold on an individual application, a batch or an entire partner, halting further progression pending investigation". So a hold can be placed at three levels, and it stops the application from moving on until the investigation is finished.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that the bank can stop suspect applications, or everything from a partner it is concerned about, without deleting anything. The row says only "pending investigation".

## Example

The Bank has a concern about one partner. Bank staff place a hold on that partner. None of the partner's applications move on until the investigation is done.

## Terms to know

- **Hold:** a stop on progression placed by Bank staff, as the BRD uses the word.
- **Batch:** a group of applications; see BR-LOS-211's batch reference.
