**In short:** Paying one or more future instalments before they fall due. It is not the same as a prepayment, which cuts the principal.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines an advance payment as payment of one or more future scheduled obligations ahead of their due dates (BRD 1.5). The money is applied to the instalments that are still to come.

## Example

Rahim owes an instalment on 5 August and another on 5 September. On 1 July he pays both amounts. The two instalments are settled early and the schedule stays as it was.

## Why it matters here

BR-LMS-180 says an advance payment is applied to future scheduled obligations according to the product's configured handling. Frappe Lending has an Advance Payment type in its restructure types, and the verified result for BR-LMS-180 is partly covered.
