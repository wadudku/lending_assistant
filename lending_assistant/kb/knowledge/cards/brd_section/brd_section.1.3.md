**In short:** Section 1.3 lists eight audiences for the BRD and the sections each should read first.

## What this section says

BRD 1.3 (PDF page 10) has a table of eight audiences with the sections of main interest: business and product owners; credit risk; finance and accounts; recovery and collections; ICT and architecture; internal control and compliance; internal audit; and supplier delivery and QA. For the last the BRD says “All. Requirements in Sections 6 to 9 form the acceptance basis.” The table is a reading guide for people with different jobs; it is not a list of requirements and it assigns no work.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that a long document is read by different people for different parts, and a pointer saves them time.

## How it meets Frappe Lending

This section holds no requirement row, so there is nothing in it to compare with Frappe Lending, and the team has made no statement about it.
