**In short:** Repricing, restructuring, settlement, waiver, write-off, bulk operations and corrective postings each need a second, different person to approve them.

## What the rule says

BR-GOV-008 (Must, BRD 3.4, PDF page 19) says every controlled financial change needs maker-checker approval by two different users. The BRD lists repricing, restructuring, settlement, waiver, write-off, bulk operation and corrective posting.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that no single person should be able to make and approve a change that moves money or alters what a customer owes.

## Example

One officer prepares a waiver of a penalty. The waiver does not take effect until a second officer, a different person, approves it.

## Terms to know

- **Maker-checker:** one person prepares a change and a different person approves it. See `glossary.maker-checker`.
- **Waiver:** the bank gives up part of what is owed. See `glossary.waiver`.
- **Restructuring:** changing the terms of a loan. See `glossary.restructuring`.
- **Corrective posting:** an entry made to fix an earlier wrong entry.
