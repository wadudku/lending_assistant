**In short:** Section 6.11 holds six requirements for administration and configuration: users and roles, product settings, versioning, reference data and moving configuration between environments.

## What this section says

BRD 6.11 (PDF page 45) holds six requirements, BR-LOS-230 to BR-LOS-235. Five are Must and one, BR-LOS-235, is Should. They cover user, role and permission management (230), product configuration (231), versioned and effective-dated configuration (232), the configuration version recorded against each decision (233), reference data (234) and export and import between environments (235).

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why for the section as a whole; the usual reason is that the bank can change settings and users itself. Two rows state a purpose on PDF page 45: BR-LOS-233 "so that a past decision can be explained by reference to the rules then applying" and BR-LOS-235 "to support controlled promotion from test to production".

## How it meets Frappe Lending

The team has not analysed the LOS (the origination system, BRD section 6), so this section carries no verdict. The analysis was limited to the 103 servicing requirements in BRD section 7. None of the six rows is among the 103 requirements the team verified. The team analysed a related servicing requirement, BR-LMS-117, and that is not a comparison with these rows.
