**In short:** The names of servicing statuses are aligned with the bank's existing vocabulary and with Bangladesh Bank classification terms before the configuration is fixed.

## What the rule says

BR-LMS-003 (Must, BRD 5.3, PDF page 28) says terminology for servicing statuses is aligned with the Bank's existing vocabulary and with Bangladesh Bank classification nomenclature before the configuration is baselined. The BRD adds a note in 5.3: the twelve status names are the supplier's proposal, and where NRB's systems or Bangladesh Bank reporting use different words for the same state, the bank's words win.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD gives the reason: the bank's own terms are adopted so that two vocabularies do not describe the same loan (PDF page 28).

## Example

The proposal calls a state SMA, but the bank's reports call it by a different local term. Before the configuration is fixed, the bank's term replaces the proposed name on every screen and report.

## Terms to know

- **Nomenclature:** the agreed set of names for things.
- **Baseline:** fix the configuration as the agreed starting version.
- **Bangladesh Bank classification:** the central bank's grades for loan quality. See `glossary.bb`.
