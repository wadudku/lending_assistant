**In short:** Section 6.10.2 holds six requirements for what partners can see: their own applications, the detail they see and do not see, disbursed accounts, reports and a dashboard.

## What this section says

BRD 6.10.2 (PDF pages 44 and 45) holds six requirements, BR-LOS-220 to BR-LOS-225. Five are Must and one, BR-LOS-225, is Should. They cover the list, filters and export (220), the application detail partners see (221), the data partners never see (222), the disbursed-accounts view (223), reports (224) and the dashboard (225).

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that partners find and support the applicants, so they need to see how their applications are moving, and only their own.

## How it meets Frappe Lending

The team has not analysed the LOS (the origination system, BRD section 6), so this section carries no verdict. The analysis was limited to the 103 servicing requirements in BRD section 7. None of the six rows is among the 103 requirements the team verified.
