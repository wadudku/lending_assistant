**In short:** Every bureau inquiry is logged in line with BR-LOS-196 and retained for the full retention period.

## What the rule says

BR-INT-033 (Must, BRD 9.5, PDF page 64) says every inquiry is logged in accordance with BR-LOS-196 and retained for the full retention period. BR-LOS-196, the row it cites, says every inquiry is logged with the application reference, initiating officer, timestamp, borrower identifier, inquiry reference, raw response, evaluated outcome and rule version, and retained for the full retention period (BRD 6.8, PDF page 42).

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that the bank must later show which inquiry was made, on whom and with what result, for audit or a dispute.

## Example

A borrower disputes a rejection. The log of the inquiry shows when it was made, the reference, the raw response and the outcome, and it is still there years later because it is kept for the full retention period.

## Terms to know

- **Inquiry log:** the record of each bureau inquiry.
- **Retention period:** how long the bank must keep a record.
