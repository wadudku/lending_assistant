**In short:** If the sanctioned amount is below the amount requested, both figures are kept, a limit-revised flag is carried, and bank staff, the partner and the field officer can all see both.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-002 (Must, BRD 5.2, PDF page 27) says that where the sanctioned amount is below the amount requested, both figures are retained, a limit-revised indicator is carried, and both figures are visible to Bank staff, the partner and the field officer. This is an origination rule (the LOS side of the BRD), not a servicing rule.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that nobody should be surprised by a lower amount. The process narrative (BRD 5.1, stage 7) says the difference is surfaced to the same three groups.

## Example

A borrower asks for 200,000 and is sanctioned 150,000. The record keeps both numbers and a flag “limit revised”. The partner and the field officer can see 200,000 asked and 150,000 sanctioned, not only the lower figure.

## Terms to know

- **Sanctioned amount:** the amount the bank agrees to lend.
- **Amount requested:** what the applicant asked for.
- **Limit-revised indicator:** a flag that says the amount was changed from the request.
- **Field officer:** the bank's staff member who meets the applicant.
