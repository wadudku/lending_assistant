**In short:** After a completed disbursement the officer sees a receipt with five items, can print and share it, and can go straight to the next applicant with the number of assignments left for the meeting event.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-208 (Must, BRD 6.9, PDF page 43) says that on completed disbursement the officer "shall be shown a receipt stating loan account number, disbursed amount, credited account, tenor and first instalment date", with print and share available. The officer "shall be able to move directly to the next applicant with a clear indication of how many assignments remain for the meeting event". So the receipt has five items.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that the applicant leaves with proof of what was paid and the officer, working through a queue of applicants (BRD 6.7, PDF page 39), can see how much of the meeting is left.

## Example

A disbursement is completed. The officer sees a receipt with the loan account number, the disbursed amount, the credited account, the tenor and the first instalment date, and prints or shares it. The officer then moves to the next applicant and sees how many assignments remain for the meeting event.

## Terms to know

- **Meeting event:** the event the BRD counts the remaining assignments for; see BR-LOS-181.
- **Credited account:** one of the five receipt items; the BRD does not define it.
