**In short:** The accounting removal of a balance the bank does not expect to recover. The borrower may still owe it, depending on policy and law.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines a write-off as the accounting removal or treatment of an unrecovered balance, and says the borrower's liability and the bank's recovery rights depend on policy and law (BRD 1.5).

## Example

After a long default, Rahim's remaining 30,000 is written off in the bank's books. If Rahim later pays 5,000, it is recorded as a recovery against the original loan.

## Why it matters here

BR-LMS-202 says a write-off must not destroy the loan's history, and BR-LMS-203 says later money is a write-off recovery on the original loan. Both are covered in the verification. See `decision.write-off` for approvals.
