**In short:** A promise by a co-lending partner to cover the first part of any losses, up to an agreed share.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines FLDG as a first-loss default guarantee: a partner guarantee absorbing an agreed first portion of losses (BRD 1.5). The plain-English guide adds that the cover is up to an agreed percentage.

## Example

A partner has an FLDG of 5 per cent on a 1,000,000 co-lent portfolio. The first 50,000 of losses is absorbed by the partner.

## Why it matters here

BR-LMS-210 asks the partner list to record the type of first-loss guarantee or other risk-sharing arrangement in force; the verified result is covered. A partner report that shows the guarantee used is part of `devitem.D19`.
