**In short:** Section 4.2.1 lists nine origination roles, two at the partner and seven at NRB Bank, with the main duty of each.

## What this section says

BRD 4.2.1 (PDF pages 21 and 22) has a table of nine roles with organisation and primary responsibility. At the partner: Partner Uploader (submit batches, correct rejected rows) and Partner Admin (manage partner users; full visibility of own portfolio only). At NRB Bank: Micro Loan Admin (sampling decisions, oversight, configuration); Sampling Reviewer (sampling decisions only); Field Officer (in-person verification, evidence capture and approval); Field Officer Supervisor (assignment, officer management, escalation, decision reversal under maker-checker); Credit Approver (sanction decisions on applications referred for human approval, within delegated authority); Credit Risk Officer (scorecard, bureau decision rules, sampling policy, mandatory sampling rules, auto-sanction thresholds); and Compliance Officer (audit trail, bureau and identity records, regulatory extracts).

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that each requirement names who may do what, and the role list gives those names a fixed meaning.

## How it meets Frappe Lending

This section holds no requirement row, so there is nothing in it to compare with Frappe Lending, and the team has made no statement about it. It describes the origination system (LOS), which the team has not analysed.
