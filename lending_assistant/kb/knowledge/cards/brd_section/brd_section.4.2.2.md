**In short:** Section 4.2.2 lists nine servicing roles at NRB Bank, from Loan Manager to Auditor, with the main duty of each.

## What this section says

BRD 4.2.2 (PDF page 22) has a table of nine roles with organisation and primary responsibility: Loan Manager (product and servicing oversight, approvals, exception management); Loan Officer (customer servicing, follow-up, authorised operational actions); Loan Processor (schedules, demands, repayments, documentation); Loan Appraiser / Underwriter (takes part where a servicing change needs renewed assessment); Collections / Recovery Officer (delinquency, promises, settlement, recovery); Finance / Accounts Officer (reconcile accruals, demands, payments, suspense and GL postings); Checker / Authoriser (approve controlled financial changes and bulk operations); Platform Administrator (user management, role assignment, system configuration); and Auditor (read-only access to all records and the audit trail; NRB Bank or external).

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that servicing actions such as waiver, restructuring and write-off need clear separation between the person who proposes and the person who approves.

## How it meets Frappe Lending

This section holds no requirement row, so there is nothing in it to compare with Frappe Lending, and the team has made no statement about it. These are the servicing roles; the team has not compared the role list with the roles of Frappe Lending.
