**In short:** Paying principal early, outside the normal schedule, so the loan shrinks. It differs from an advance payment, which pays future instalments early.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines prepayment as early reduction of outstanding principal outside the normal schedule (BRD 1.5).

## Example

Rahim has 80,000 of principal left and pays an extra 20,000 this month. The principal falls to 60,000. Either his instalment gets smaller or the loan ends sooner, depending on what he chose.

## Why it matters here

BR-LMS-181 asks that a prepayment reduces principal, applies any configured charge, and revises either the instalment or the tenor by the borrower's approved choice. In the test, a Pre Payment always kept the EMI and shortened the loan, and there was no choice. The result is partly covered.
