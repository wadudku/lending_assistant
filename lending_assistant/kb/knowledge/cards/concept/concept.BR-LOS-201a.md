**In short:** If funding cannot finish within the configured waiting period, the officer can end the visit at approved status, funding completes later, and both the borrower and the officer are told the outcome.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-201a (Must, BRD 6.9, PDF page 43) says that where the automation cannot complete funding "within the configured field waiting period", the officer "shall be able to conclude the visit at approved status". "Funding shall then complete asynchronously and both the borrower and the officer shall be notified of the outcome." The last sentence reads "The officer shall never be left waiting on an indefinite progress indicator."

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why beyond the row's last sentence, which says the officer must never wait on an indefinite progress indicator; the usual reason is that an officer at a gathering with a queue of applicants (BRD 6.7, PDF page 39) cannot wait without end for the automation.

## Example

The automation has not finished funding by the end of the configured waiting period. The officer ends the visit with the application at approved status and moves on. Funding finishes later, and the borrower and the officer are both notified of the outcome.

## Terms to know

- **Field waiting period:** the configured time the officer waits for funding; the BRD gives no figure.
- **Asynchronously:** later, without the officer waiting, as the BRD uses the word.
