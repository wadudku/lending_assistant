**In short:** The two sides of an accounting entry. Each posting debits one account and credits another.

## Plain meaning

Cr means credit and Dr means debit. The plain-English guide calls them the two sides of an accounting entry: every posting debits one account and credits another.

## Example

Rahim repays 9,000 in cash. The bank debits its cash account by 9,000 and credits the loan account by 9,000.

## Why it matters here

The verification notes accounting entries in this form, for example a waiver or a capitalisation shown as a debit to one account and a credit to another. Reading them needs this pair of words.
