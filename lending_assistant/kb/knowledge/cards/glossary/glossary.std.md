**In short:** A healthy loan, paid on time or only slightly late.

## Plain meaning

The plain-English guide defines STD as Standard: a healthy loan, paid on time or only slightly late. In its sample bands, Standard is 0 to 59 days past due.

## Example

Rahim pays his instalment 5 days late each month. In the guide's sample bands the loan stays Standard.

## Why it matters here

BR-LMS-191 asks that the class is assigned from configured days-past-due bands, so the limits of Standard are the bank's choice.
