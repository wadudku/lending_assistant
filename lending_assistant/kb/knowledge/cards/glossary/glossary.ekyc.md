**In short:** Identity checking on a phone or tablet: a check against the national database, a liveness test and a face match.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines eKYC as Electronic Know Your Customer: identity verification against the national database together with liveness detection and face matching (BRD 1.5).

## Example

At the visit, the field officer checks the applicant's details, and the app takes a live photo and checks it is a live person, then compares it with the portrait from the national database.

## Why it matters here

eKYC is part of the Field Officer App in scope (BRD 1.2.1) and of field verification (BRD 5.1, stage 9). The team has analysed only the LMS requirements (Section 7). The LOS requirements have not been analysed.
