**In short:** Partners can generate and schedule reports on batch summary, approval funnel, rejection reasons by category and disbursement register, exportable to XLSX and CSV.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-224 (Must, BRD 6.10.2, PDF page 45) says partners "shall be able to generate and schedule reports covering batch summary, approval funnel, rejection reasons by category and disbursement register, exportable to XLSX and CSV". That is four reports and two export formats. So a partner can ask for a report when it wants one and can also set it to be produced on a schedule.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that partners track their own pipeline and want regular reports without asking the bank for them.

## Example

A partner schedules the disbursement register to be produced regularly and exports the result to XLSX. Later the partner generates the rejection reasons by category on request and exports that report to CSV.

## Terms to know

- **Approval funnel:** the BRD's term for how applications progress towards approval; it does not define it further.
- **Disbursement register:** the BRD's name for a list of disbursements.
