**In short:** Section 6.6 holds fourteen rules for sanction: how the proposed terms are set, automatic sanction inside thresholds, a credit approval queue, the approver's choices, authority limits, conditions, records and reports.

## What this section says

BRD 6.6 (PDF pages 37 to 39) says sanction is the Bank's decision to lend, on stated terms, to a named applicant. It sits in the origination system, not in servicing: the LMS receives a loan that has already been sanctioned and funded. The Bank's position is that the scorecard and product configuration should carry the routine cases, and a credit officer should see everything above that line; the threshold is configuration, and sanction occurs before assignment. It holds fourteen requirements, BR-LOS-320 to BR-LOS-333: the proposed sanction is set from product, grade, headroom and exposure (320); automatic sanction only inside every threshold (321); outside a threshold the case waits in a credit approval queue (322); the approver sees the whole case on one screen (323); the approver has five ways to decide (324); delegated authority is enforced at the moment of decision (325); approvers cannot approve their own sampled cases or their own organisation's (326); conditions on a sanction are recorded and evidenced (327); every sanction is recorded as a system or person's decision (328); cases awaiting approval age visibly and escalate (329); a sanction lapses after a set period (330); the borrower's total exposure is added up and held to a limit (331); no sanction if identity failed, a validation is outstanding or the ceiling would break (332); and sanction activity is reported (333).

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD gives the reason in the section introduction: the scorecard and product configuration carry the routine cases, a credit officer sees everything above the threshold, and sanction comes before assignment so that no field officer is sent to meet an applicant the Bank has not agreed to lend to (PDF page 37).

## How it meets Frappe Lending

The section holds 14 requirements (BR-LOS-320 to BR-LOS-333). The team has not analysed them: the analysis covered the 103 servicing requirements of BRD section 7, so this card gives no verdict. Each row has a concept card with the rule and a not-analysed bridge card with pointers only.
