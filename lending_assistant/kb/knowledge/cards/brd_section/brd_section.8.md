**In short:** Section 8 sets out what reporting the platform must provide: origination and servicing reports, Bangladesh Bank returns and reconciliation.

## What this section says

BRD 8 (PDF page 57) opens with the statement that “Reporting is not a by-product of this programme; for several stakeholders it is the programme.” It names four groups that each need a view the current process cannot produce reliably at volume: Credit Risk, Finance, Compliance and Internal Audit. It holds four numbered sub-sections, 8.1 to 8.4, with 16 requirement rows in all (3 in 8.1, 3 in 8.2, 5 in 8.3 and 5 in 8.4). Section 8 has no row of its own.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD gives its reason in the first paragraph: these four groups “each need a view that the current process cannot produce reliably at volume” (PDF page 57).

## How it meets Frappe Lending

The team's analysis covered Section 7 only. This section was not analysed, so the team makes no statement about it. For the Section 7 totals, see `brd_section.7`.
