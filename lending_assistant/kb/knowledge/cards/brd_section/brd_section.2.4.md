**In short:** Section 2.4 sets nine success measures with targets, such as zero misdirected disbursements and batch validation in under ten minutes.

## What this section says

BRD 2.4 (PDF pages 15 and 16) has a table of nine measures with definition and target. Batch validation turnaround: under 10 minutes from partner upload to results for a 500-row file. Origination cycle time: from submission to confirmed disbursement for an application that passes every check first time; target “Same meeting event”. Duplicate or misdirected disbursement: zero. Reconciliation integrity: zero unexplained differences with CBS or LMS records carried overnight. Sampling rejection rate: monitored, threshold-driven. Schedule generation accuracy: zero repayment schedules needing manual correction. Classification and provisioning basis: zero loans whose classification or provision cannot be explained by recorded DPD, configuration version and rule version. Regulatory return preparation: a system-generated extract for the agricultural credit return and monthly CIB contribution. Field officer throughput: verifications per officer in a standard meeting event, to be set with Operations.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that a measure with a target tells the Bank, after go-live, whether the programme worked (PDF page 15).

## How it meets Frappe Lending

This section holds no requirement row, so there is nothing in it to compare with Frappe Lending, and the team has made no statement about it. It also describes the origination system (LOS), which the team has not analysed.
