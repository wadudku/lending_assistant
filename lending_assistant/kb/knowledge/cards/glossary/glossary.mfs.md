**In short:** Mobile money such as bKash or Nagad, one of the ways a borrower might repay.

## Plain meaning

The plain-English guide defines MFS as mobile financial services, such as bKash or Nagad, and says it is one of the possible repayment channels in BRD 7.7.2. The BRD lists it with standing instruction, partner bulk remittance, branch or agent cash and interbank transfer.

## Example

Rahim sends his EMI from his mobile wallet. The credit reaches the bank with a reference. The bank still has to match it to Rahim's loan.

## Why it matters here

BRD 7.7.2 does not decide which channels carry the product; it asks NRB. Each channel behaves differently and carries a different attribution problem, so the answer shapes the design of payment matching.
