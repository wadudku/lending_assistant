**In short:** The first non-performing class. In the guide's example bands it starts at 90 days past due.

## Plain meaning

The plain-English guide defines SS as Sub-Standard, the first NPA class, and gives 90 to 179 days past due in its sample bands.

## Example

In the sample bands, Rahim's loan at 95 days past due is Sub-Standard. The bank's real bands are set in configuration.

## Why it matters here

Sub-Standard is where, in the sample bands, a loan crosses from the early-warning class into non-performing status. See `lifecycle.NPA` for what the BRD asks to happen then.
