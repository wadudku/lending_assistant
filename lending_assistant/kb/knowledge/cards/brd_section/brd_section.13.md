**In short:** Section 13 is a table of 19 risks, each with a likelihood and impact rating and a mitigation.

## What this section says

BRD 13 (PDF pages 72 to 74) is a table of 19 risks, numbered R1 to R15 with the sub-numbers R1a, R1b, R13a and R13b. Each row gives the risk, a likelihood and impact pair and a mitigation. The ratings run from Low to Very high. For example, risk R10, “Scope expands through informal request during build”, is rated High / Medium. Its mitigation reads “Baseline this document; route every change through Section 15 change control with impact assessment on cost and timeline.” (PDF page 73). Nine of the 19 mitigations cite requirement ids, eight of them from Sections 6 and 9 (R9 cites BR-GOV-012). Three cite a dependency of Section 12, and R12 covers the Section 12 dependencies as a whole.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that a delivery plan is stronger when each known risk has a named response, and the bank can see which requirements are there to answer them.

## How it meets Frappe Lending

This section holds no requirement row, so there is nothing in it to compare with Frappe Lending, and the team has made no statement about it.
