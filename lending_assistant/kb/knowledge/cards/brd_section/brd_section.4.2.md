**In short:** Section 4.2 is a heading only; the origination and servicing roles are in its two sub-sections.

## What this section says

BRD 4.2 (PDF page 21) is a title with no text of its own. The roles are set out in 4.2.1 Origination roles (PDF pages 21 and 22) and 4.2.2 Servicing roles (PDF page 22). This section holds no requirement. Read with 4.3, the roles are the people to whom the access and segregation rules apply. The section holds no text between the title and its two sub-sections.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that origination and servicing are run by different groups of people, so the roles are listed separately.

## How it meets Frappe Lending

This heading has no text of its own and no requirement row, so there is nothing to compare with Frappe Lending, and the team has made no statement about it.
