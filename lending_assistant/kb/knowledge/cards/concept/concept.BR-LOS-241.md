**In short:** The platform has a standard set of six origination reports, from a batch summary to a disbursement register.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-241 (Must, BRD 8.1, PDF page 57) says the standard origination reports include six: a batch summary; an approval funnel; rejection reasons by category; sampling outcomes by partner and reviewer; verification outcomes by officer; and the disbursement register.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD gives one general reason for the whole of Section 8: “Reporting is not a by-product of this programme; for several stakeholders it is the programme. Credit Risk, Finance, Compliance and Internal Audit each need a view that the current process cannot produce reliably at volume.” (PDF page 57). The BRD doesn't say why these six; the usual reason is that each one answers a question that credit, operations and audit ask regularly, so it is built once and not compiled by hand.

## Example

At month end an auditor asks why applications were turned down. The rejection-reasons report lists them by category, for example 40 for incomplete documents and 15 for income too low, and the same report is run again next month with the same layout.

## Terms to know

- **Batch summary:** a count and value of the applications that came in together in one batch from a partner.
- **Approval funnel:** the steps an application passes through towards approval, and how many pass each step.
- **Sampling:** checking a chosen part of the applications instead of all of them.
- **Disbursement register:** the list of payouts made.
