**In short:** A bank staff member who meets applicants in person at a partner's gathering to verify them.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines a Field Officer as a Bank staff member conducting in-person verification at a partner meeting (BRD 1.5).

## Example

A field officer travels to a village meeting, confirms the details of each approved applicant, captures a signature and fingerprint, and confirms the loan terms with them.

## Why it matters here

The field officer is a role in the origination process (BRD 4.2.1). The device must never hold the score or its logic (BR-LOS-314). The team has analysed only the LMS requirements (Section 7). The LOS requirements have not been analysed.
