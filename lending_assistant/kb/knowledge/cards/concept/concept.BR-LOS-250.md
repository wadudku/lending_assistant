**In short:** The platform produces the data extracts the bank needs for Bangladesh Bank agricultural and rural credit reporting; the bank is to supply the formats.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-250 (Must, BRD 8.3, PDF page 58) says the platform supports the data extracts the bank requires for Bangladesh Bank agricultural and rural credit reporting. The required formats are to be supplied by the bank. The BRD does not list the fields or the format itself.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why this return is needed. A note on PDF page 58 says the regulatory obligations are the Bank's to set, not the supplier's, and that the Bank supplies the rule set, the return formats and the submission calendar.

## Example

The bank sends the extract layout it must file for agricultural lending: one row per loan with the borrower, crop, amount and outstanding balance. The platform produces a file in that layout for the reporting month, and the bank files it with Bangladesh Bank.

## Terms to know

- **Extract:** a file of selected data in a fixed layout.
- **Bangladesh Bank:** the central bank and regulator. See `glossary.bb`.
