**In short:** Each field officer has a profile with nine items, including coverage areas, the bound device and effective dates.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-184 (Must, BRD 6.7.4, PDF page 41) says field officer profiles "shall record" nine items: employee identifier, name, branch, designation, contact details, coverage areas, bound device identifier, status and effective dates.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that assigning, device binding and access control all need a reliable record of who each officer is, which device they hold and for what dates the profile is in force.

## Example

A new field officer is set up with an employee identifier, name, branch, designation and contact details, the areas the officer covers and the device bound to them. The profile has a status and the dates from which it applies.

## Terms to know

- **Bound device identifier:** the identifier of the device the officer is bound to (BR-LOS-151).
- **Effective dates:** an item the BRD lists on the profile; it gives no further detail.
