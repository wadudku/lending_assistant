**In short:** Chapter 1 is the introduction; it holds the purpose, scope, audience, reading guide and definitions in its five sections.

## What this section says

BRD 1 (PDF page 9) is a heading with no text of its own. Its sections are 1.1 Purpose (PDF page 9), 1.2 Scope of this document (PDF page 9, with 1.2.1 In scope and 1.2.2 Explicitly out of scope), 1.3 Intended audience (PDF page 10), 1.4 How to read this document (PDF pages 10 and 11) and 1.5 Definitions and abbreviations (PDF pages 11 to 13). This chapter holds no requirement.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that a requirements document starts by saying what it is for, what it covers and how to read it.

## How it meets Frappe Lending

This heading has no text of its own and no requirement row, so there is nothing to compare with Frappe Lending, and the team has made no statement about it.
