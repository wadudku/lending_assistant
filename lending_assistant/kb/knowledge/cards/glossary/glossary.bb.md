**In short:** Bangladesh's central bank and regulator. It sets the classification, provisioning and reporting rules the bank follows.

## Plain meaning

The plain-English guide defines BB as Bangladesh Bank, Bangladesh's central bank and regulator, which sets classification, provisioning and reporting rules.

## Example

When the BRD says unpaid interest moves to suspense in line with Bangladesh Bank rules, the BB rules apply to that move.

## Why it matters here

Several requirements point to BB rules: BR-LMS-144 (suspense), BR-LMS-188 (reporting restructures, reschedules and waivers). The team does not yet have the list of BB circulars, which it lists as an open item for NRB.
