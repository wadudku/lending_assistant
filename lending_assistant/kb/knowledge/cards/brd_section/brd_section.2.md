**In short:** Chapter 2 gives the business context: the current state, the rationale, the objectives and the success measures.

## What this section says

BRD 2 (PDF page 14) is a heading with no text of its own. Its sections are 2.1 Current state (PDF page 14), 2.2 Business rationale (PDF page 14), 2.3 Business objectives (PDF pages 14 and 15) and 2.4 Success measures (PDF pages 15 and 16). This chapter holds no requirement. Read in order, the four sections move from the problem (what is wrong today), to the reason for the programme, to what it must achieve, to how success will be measured.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that a requirements document explains the business problem before it lists what to build.
