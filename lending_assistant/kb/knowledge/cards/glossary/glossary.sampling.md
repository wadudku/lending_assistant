**In short:** Manual review of a randomly selected part of the submitted applications.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines sampling as manual review of a randomly selected subset of submitted applications (BRD 1.5).

## Example

Out of 100 scored applications, 10 are picked at random for a person to review, together with every referred case and every case caught by a mandatory rule. The other 90 pass automatically.

## Why it matters here

Sampling is how the Bank keeps credit judgement over a volume too large to review one by one (BRD 6.5). The rate is set globally, per partner and per product (BR-LOS-135). The team has analysed only the LMS requirements (Section 7). The LOS requirements have not been analysed.
