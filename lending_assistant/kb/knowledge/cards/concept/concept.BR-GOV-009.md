**In short:** Repayment, schedule and accounting histories cannot be changed or deleted; a mistake is fixed by a controlled reversal and a new entry, and the original stays.

## What the rule says

BR-GOV-009 (Must, BRD 3.4, PDF page 19) says repayment, schedule and accounting histories are immutable. A correction is made through a controlled reversal and re-entry that preserves the original, never through silent amendment or deletion.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that an audit needs to see what was recorded at the time and how it was later corrected, which is lost if the old entry is overwritten.

## Example

A repayment of 5,000 was entered as 500. The wrong entry is reversed, so it still shows in the history together with its reversal, and a correct entry of 5,000 is added.

## Terms to know

- **Immutable:** cannot be changed after it is recorded.
- **Reversal:** an entry that cancels an earlier one without erasing it.
- **Re-entry:** recording the correct entry after the reversal.
- **Silent amendment:** changing a record without leaving a trace of the change.
