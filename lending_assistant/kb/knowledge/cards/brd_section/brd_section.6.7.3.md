**In short:** Section 6.7.3 holds nine requirements for how an officer starts a verification and captures evidence, from KYC confirmation to the audit trail.

## What this section says

BRD 6.7.3 (PDF pages 40 and 41) holds nine requirements, BR-LOS-170 to BR-LOS-178, all Must. They cover starting a verification from the list or by manual lookup (170), confirming the KYC data or flagging a discrepancy (171), supplementary fields (172), a wet signature (173), a fingerprint (174), a liveness check (175), the details recorded with every capture (176), the limit on liveness attempts (177) and evidence that cannot be changed once synchronised (178).

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD gives no separate reason for 6.7.3. The context for all of 6.7 is on PDF page 39: officers work in rural places with unreliable mobile data, standing and under time pressure. The BRD doesn't say why each capture is needed; the usual reason is that together they show who the applicant is and that they were present.

## How it meets Frappe Lending

The team has not analysed the LOS (the origination system, BRD section 6), so this section carries no verdict. The analysis was limited to the 103 servicing requirements in BRD section 7. None of the nine rows is among the 103 requirements the team verified.
