**In short:** A fixed deposit is a term deposit. Its receipt, the FDR, can be pledged as collateral or held as a partner's guarantee money.

## Plain meaning

The plain-English guide defines an FD as a term deposit, here used as guarantee money a co-lending partner puts up. An FDR is the certificate for a fixed deposit, used as collateral in one of the practice tests (C5).

## Example

Rahim pledges a fixed deposit receipt for 100,000 as security for his loan. The receipt is the collateral the bank holds.

## Why it matters here

BRD 7.8 is about collateral and security servicing. The analysis tested a pledged FDR in its collateral example C5.
