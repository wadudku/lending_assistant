**In short:** A gathering arranged by a partner where eligible applicants and Bank field officers are both present.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines a meeting event as a gathering arranged by a partner at which eligible applicants and Bank field officers are both present (BRD 1.5).

## Example

A partner arranges a meeting at a village hall on a Saturday. Forty sanctioned applicants and three field officers attend, and verification happens there.

## Why it matters here

Applications are grouped by meeting event for assignment (BRD 5.1, stage 8). The target for origination cycle time is 'same meeting event' (BRD 2.4), and approval queues must not silently delay one (BR-LOS-329). The team has analysed only the LMS requirements (Section 7). The LOS requirements have not been analysed.
