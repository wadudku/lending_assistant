**In short:** Chapter 6 states what the origination system must do; it is a long chapter with eleven sections, and its depth is deliberately uneven.

## What this section says

BRD 6 (PDF page 29) opens: “This section states what the origination system must do for the business. Requirement depth is deliberately uneven: flows that carry financial, regulatory or reputational risk are specified with their business rules and acceptance expectations; supporting functions are stated as requirements to be elaborated at design.” The chapter has eleven numbered sections, 6.1 to 6.11. This knowledge base describes 6.1 to 6.6 in this part: partner and product setup, bulk application submission, pre-submission account validation, application scoring, sampling and credit oversight, and sanction and credit approval. Sections 6.7 to 6.11 follow in a later part.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD gives the reason for the uneven depth in the opening text: flows that carry financial, regulatory or reputational risk get business rules and acceptance expectations, and supporting functions are left to be elaborated at design (PDF page 29).

## How it meets Frappe Lending

This heading holds an introduction and no requirement row. The chapter describes the origination system (LOS), which the team has not analysed, so there is nothing here to compare with Frappe Lending and the team has made no statement about it.
