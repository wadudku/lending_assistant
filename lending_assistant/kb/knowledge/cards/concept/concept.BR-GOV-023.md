**In short:** A sampling reviewer cannot review an application sent by an organisation their account is linked to, or one they have decided before.

## What the rule says

BR-GOV-023 (Must, BRD 4.3, PDF page 23) says a sampling reviewer cannot review an application submitted by an organisation to which their user account is linked, nor one they have previously decided.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is conflict of interest: a reviewer should not judge a case from their own organisation, and should not decide the same case twice.

## Example

A reviewer whose account is linked to partner A is shown a batch drawn for sampling. Applications from partner A are not offered to that reviewer, and a case the reviewer has already decided does not come back to them.

## Terms to know

- **Sampling:** checking a share of applications, picked by rule, instead of all of them.
- **Reviewer:** the person who checks a sampled application.
- **Linked organisation:** the organisation a user account belongs to.
