**In short:** All configuration is versioned and effective-dated, with a change history showing before value, after value, maker, checker, timestamp and justification.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-232 (Must, BRD 6.11, PDF page 45) says "All configuration shall be versioned and effective-dated, with a change history showing before value, after value, maker, checker, timestamp and justification." The change history has six items.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that every change to a setting can be traced to who made and approved it, and when it took effect. BR-LOS-233 states a use for the versions.

## Example

A rate in a product's configuration is changed. The change history keeps the old value, the new value, who made the change, who approved it, when, and the reason given. The change applies from its effective date.

## Terms to know

- **Effective-dated:** applies from a stated date, as the BRD uses the term.
- **Justification:** the last of the six items in the change history.
