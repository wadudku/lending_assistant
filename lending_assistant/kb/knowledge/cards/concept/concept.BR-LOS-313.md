**In short:** The Bank can reproduce, for any past application, the score, attribute values, grade, cut-offs and scorecard version in force at decision time, whatever changed later.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-313 (Must, BRD 6.4, PDF page 35) says the Bank can reproduce, for any past application, the score, the attribute values, the grade, the cut-offs and the scorecard version in force at its own decision time, irrespective of later scorecard changes. This is an origination rule (the LOS side of the BRD), not a servicing rule.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD gives the reason in the 6.5 introduction: decisions that are attributable and reproducible are what make partner-sourced origination defensible to internal audit and to the regulator (PDF page 35).

## Example

A year after a decision, auditors ask why an application was approved. The Bank shows the score, the inputs, the grade and the cut-offs as they were then, even though the scorecard has changed twice since.

## Terms to know

- **Reproduce:** show again exactly as it was.
- **Decision time:** the moment the decision was made.
- **Scorecard version:** the numbered edition of the scorecard in use.
- **Irrespective of:** regardless of.
