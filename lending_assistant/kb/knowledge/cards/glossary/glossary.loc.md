**In short:** A revolving facility with an approved limit that the borrower can draw on, repay and draw on again.

## Plain meaning

The BRD defines a line of credit as a revolving facility with an approved utilisation limit (BRD 1.5).

## Example

Rahim has a line of credit with a 200,000 limit. He draws 50,000, repays 20,000 and can then draw again within the limit.

## Why it matters here

BR-LMS-110 lists the revolving line of credit among the schedule methods, and BR-LMS-221 asks for credit-line limits and a log of every limit change with its reason and approver. The verified result for BR-LMS-221 is partly covered: the log has no reason or approver.
