**In short:** Product configuration is to include product code and name, minimum and maximum ticket, permitted tenors, rate and fee structure, eligibility rules and required application fields.

## What the rule says

BR-LOS-231 (Must, BRD 6.11, PDF page 45) says product configuration "shall cover product code and name, minimum and maximum ticket, permitted tenors, rate and fee structure, eligibility rules and required application fields".

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that the bank can set up or change a loan product by configuration, without changing the software.

## Example

The Bank sets up a product with a code and name, a minimum and maximum ticket, the tenors allowed, its rate and fees, its eligibility rules and the application fields it requires.

## Terms to know

- **Ticket:** the BRD's word for the thing that has a minimum and a maximum; it does not define it.
- **Eligibility rules:** one of the items product configuration covers, as the BRD lists it; it does not define it.
