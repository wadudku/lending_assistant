**In short:** Section 8.1 asks for an origination dashboard, a standard set of origination reports, and a way to export, schedule and email reports.

## What this section says

BRD 8.1 (PDF page 57) has 3 requirements, all Must. BR-LOS-240: an operational dashboard showing pipeline volume and value by status, funnel conversion and drop-off, ageing within status, a partner league table and field officer utilisation. BR-LOS-241: standard origination reports, six of them named: batch summary, approval funnel, rejection reasons by category, sampling outcomes by partner and reviewer, verification outcomes by officer, and the disbursement register. BR-LOS-242: reports exportable to XLSX and CSV, schedulable, and deliverable to configured recipients by email.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD gives one reason for all of Section 8 on PDF page 57: Credit Risk, Finance, Compliance and Internal Audit each need a view that the current process cannot produce reliably at volume. It doesn't say why 8.1 in particular asks for these three.

## How it meets Frappe Lending

The team's analysis covered Section 7 only. This section holds 3 requirement rows and none was analysed, so the team makes no statement about them. For the Section 7 totals, see `brd_section.7`.
