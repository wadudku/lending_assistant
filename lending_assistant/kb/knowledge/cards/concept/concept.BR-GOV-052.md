**In short:** Each reconciliation break can be assigned to an owner and needs a documented closure; open breaks age visibly and are reported to management after a configurable period.

## What the rule says

BR-GOV-052 (Must, BRD 8.4, PDF page 59) says each reconciliation break is assignable to an owner and requires a documented closure. Open breaks age visibly and are reported to management after a configurable period.

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that a difference nobody owns is never fixed, and one that is closed without a reason cannot be defended to an auditor.

## Example

A break is assigned to an operations officer on Monday. By Friday it is shown as five days old. After the configured seven days it is included in the report to management. The officer closes it with a note saying a payment had been posted twice and one posting was reversed.

## Terms to know

- **Break:** a difference found by a reconciliation.
- **Documented closure:** closing a break with a written reason.
- **Ageing:** showing how long an item has been open.
