**In short:** Section 6.7.4 holds seven requirements on assigning sanctioned applications to field officers and managing the officers.

## What this section says

BRD 6.7.4 (PDF pages 41 and 42) holds seven requirements, BR-LOS-180 to BR-LOS-186. Six are Must and one is Should (BR-LOS-186). They cover the assignment queue (180), the meeting event record (181), bulk assignment with a workload view and a capacity warning (182), reassignment and automatic return to the queue (183), the field officer profile (184), revoking access on suspension (185) and officer performance reporting (186).

## Why the bank wants it

The BRD doesn't say why; the usual reason is that sanctioned applicants are visited at meetings, so the bank needs to plan who goes where and to remove an officer's access when they leave.

## How it meets Frappe Lending

The team has not analysed the LOS (the origination system, BRD section 6), so this section carries no verdict. The analysis was limited to the 103 servicing requirements in BRD section 7. None of the seven rows is among the 103 requirements the team verified.
